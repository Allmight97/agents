What would settle the proposal

Can search recover the useful notes?Try real questions against a small representative set.

Choose notes that were previously hard to find. Test whether their titles and context are sufficient for retrieval, and inspect misses before introducing a tagging system.

Evidence needed: the questions used, notes recovered, and reasons relevant notes were missed.

Does maintaining the index earn its time?Measure the recurring work as well as the retrieval benefit.

Try adding and revising notes through the proposed workflow. Keep only fields whose retrieval value justifies maintaining them.

Decision: continue with a minimal index if it helps; reconsider the organization if routine maintenance dominates the benefit.
